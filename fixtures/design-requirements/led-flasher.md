# Nine volt LED flasher

Design a compact board powered by a nominal 9 V DC input that flashes one visible
LED at roughly one flash per second using a 555 timer. Provide a two-pin power
connector, a current-limiting resistor for the LED, supply bypassing and the timer
support components. Choose sensible timing values and stocked parts. No external
clock or microcontroller is wanted. Label input polarity. Frequency, LED current
and behavior over the battery voltage range need measurement after assembly.
