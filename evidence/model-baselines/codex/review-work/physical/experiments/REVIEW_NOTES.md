## Measurements, calculations and interpretation

Five unnumbered J1 pads specify thru_hole, *.Cu and pad diameter equal drill diameter (three 4.0 mm, two 2.2 mm). Annular ring calculation (pad diameter - drill diameter)/2 = 0 mm; saved project rule min_via_annular_width is 0.1 mm. Each has two excluded native errors with empty exclusion comment. Their lack of a net prevents interpreting them as a demonstrated signal open. The physical finding concerns native manufacturing geometry and the actual plated drill classification; no fab rejection or mechanical failure is asserted.

C15.2, U7.4, C14.2 are GND pads in zone bc540b8b-3ffa-4b00-b211-3a91191a1f6d. Both supplied and independent DRC identify one F.Cu spoke, versus min_resolved_spokes=2. Native ground zone has 0.3 mm thermal gap and 0.4 mm bridge setting. All three are explicitly excluded without comments. This is a saved-rule exception, not proof of an open circuit, inadequate ampacity or an overheating component. Zero unconnected items does not verify spoke redundancy.

R36 and R37 are each 120 ohms, DNP and excluded from schematic BOM; saved PCB also marks them DNP. Each spans CAN2+ and CAN2-. Both WBO transceivers, J2.73/.74 and J1.15/.11 share the bus. Default carrier resistor conductance is 0 S; one fitted resistor would give 120 ohms and two would give 60 ohms in parallel. These are resistor-population calculations, not measured total bus impedance: brain-module and external harness termination are outside supplied native carrier implementation. An isolated carrier could lack resistive termination; a correctly terminated external/module bus could be entirely intentional. This uncertainty is a concern only, with no categorical claim that the short internal bus fails.

`python3 current_path_calculation.py > current_path_calculation.log` records a conditional resistance calculation from saved B.Cu heater-negative chains. Excluding the module pad11-pad12 stub under the explicit equipotential assumption, paths are 109.9709 mm and 156.481 mm, 0.6 mm wide. With saved 35 um copper and assumed copper resistivity 1.724e-8 ohm*m at 20 C, R=rho*L/(w*t) gives 0.09028 and 0.12846 ohms. V=IR and P=I^2 R are provided as coefficients only. No load current, thermal boundary, contact/barrel resistance, copper tolerance or operating temperature has been established. No ampacity, temperature rise or thermal defect is inferred from these widths or resistances.
