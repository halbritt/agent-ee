### ★ Focus #1: `B3-1` — Sandboxed Counterexample Witness Replay and Check-Promotion Receipts `[N9 V10 F8 = 9.15]` *(superseded #3: N9/Val8/Feas10 = 8.85)*
*Merged cluster representatives*: `B3-1, B4-1, B5-5, B1-5, B2-2`

#### 1. Focus Branch Sketch (Isolated Subagent Output)
When a `circuit` or `physical` reviewer discovers an unmodeled defect during `review_role()`, they author a self-contained executable counterexample script or SPICE/pcbnew harness under `/work/experiments/<witness_id>/` and reference its relative path in a structured `witness` descriptor on their `concern` finding. Before `adjudicate_findings()` runs, the coordinator inspects every `major` or `blocking` reviewer `concern` carrying a `witness` descriptor and copies only that witness directory into a fresh, ephemeral, networkless check sandbox (`run_in_sandbox` with `allow_network=False`) alongside a read-only mount of the sealed `/candidate` snapshot. The coordinator executes the witness entrypoint under deterministic resource and timeout limits against `/candidate` (and optionally `/baseline` for differential classification) and records a SHA-256-sealed receipt under `checks/witness-<role>-<id>/result.json`. In `findings.py` and `baseline.py`, `agent-ee.scope/2` retains its strict ban on accepting unverified prose, while permitting a reviewer discovery backed by a sealed, deterministically failing witness receipt to be promoted into an executable check citation or recorded as a machine-verified check-promotion receipt in `tradeoff.json`. When the campaign halts on `unresolved_review_claims` or advances to a new iteration, `tradeoff.json` emits the exact command, artifact digests, and reproduction receipt needed to graduate the witness into `scope.checks`.

- **Load-Bearing Risk**: False-positive or brittle reviewer witnesses—such as a SPICE deck with non-convergent solver settings or a script that crashes on missing imports or hardcoded `/work` paths—could fail with a non-zero exit code for reasons unrelated to the claimed hardware defect, tricking the coordinator into validating a broken script as a confirmed hardware counterexample unless assertion failures are strictly separated from harness runtime errors.
- **First Concrete Step**: Extend the reviewer finding validator in `src/agent_ee/findings.py` to accept an optional `witness` object (`{"entrypoint": "experiments/...", "kind": "spice|pcbnew|python"}`) on `concern` findings, and add a `replay_reviewer_witnesses()` helper in `src/agent_ee/campaign.py` between `review()` and `adjudicate_findings()` that runs each declared entrypoint against the read-only `/candidate` mount via `run_in_sandbox(..., allow_network=False)` and writes `checks/witness-<id>/result.json`.
- **Child Ideas Unlocked**:
  - **B3-1a — Differential Witness Replay Across `/baseline` and `/candidate`**: Replay every reviewer witness against both read-only `/baseline` and `/candidate` mounts in the networkless sandbox; a witness that passes on `/baseline` and fails on `/candidate` proves an iteration-induced regression that can be immediately promoted to an in-loop check for the next `implement()` pass.
  - **B3-1b — Tri-State Witness Exit Contract (Pass / Counterexample / Harness Error)**: Enforce a strict witness protocol where exit code 1 plus a schema-valid `counterexample.json` artifact denotes a verified hardware defect, whereas exit codes >=2 or missing structured output classify the witness as a broken harness (`discarded` during adjudication) so buggy reviewer scripts cannot stall convergence.
  - **B3-1c — Turnkey `scope.checks` Graduation Bundle in `tradeoff.json`**: When halting on a witness-verified `unresolved_review_claims` finding, package the sanitized witness script into the iteration output and emit a ready-to-merge `scope.checks` descriptor in `tradeoff.json` so the outer orchestrator or human engineer can promote the check with zero manual script extraction.
  - **B3-1d — Design-Coupling Provenance Check for Witnesses**: Prevent trivial `exit 1` witness gaming by requiring the witness execution in the check sandbox to read at least one `/candidate` schematic, netlist, or PCB file (verified via read-access trace or required SHA-256 + extracted net/component metrics in `counterexample.json`) before its failure receipt is considered valid.
