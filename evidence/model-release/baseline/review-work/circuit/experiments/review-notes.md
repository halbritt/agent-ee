## Findings basis and rejected overclaims

CAN2: native wbo-can-trans.kicad_sch resistor UUID d69380f9-5487-4782-9ce9-0690df62e543 has value 120, DNP yes, excluded from BOM. Its two sheet instances are R36 and R37. Both native board footprints also have DNP attributes and pads on /CAN2+ and /CAN2-. No populated direct resistor across these two nets exists on the carrier. The two transceiver input resistances are not replacements for termination. J2.73/74 and J1.15/11 expose the bus to unknown module/harness termination; absence of a documented complete termination configuration is a concern, not a demonstrated CAN failure. No bus rate, topology impedance, transmitted waveform, receiver sampling measurement or passive DC resistance measurement was supplied.

Lowside drive: all eight VNL SOT-223 inputs are fed from GPIO interface nets via 1 kohm RN2/RN4 branches, with 1 kohm RN1/RN3 pulls to ground at the GPIO nets. There is no 5 V buffer. LS9/10 additionally have native 1 kohm pulls to 5 V, yielding 2.5 V unloaded when GPIOs float. For a 3.3 V push-pull GPIO, the driver input is at most 3.3 V before series-resistor drop. Both possible N3 part identities specify 3.5 V minimum control supply. This shows missing guaranteed drive margin under that conditional module configuration; it does not establish that all samples fail or quantify load current. At reset the nonzero LS9/10 bias likewise has no demonstrated safe-off waveform. These uncertainties are retained in the concern.

U11–U19 pad 2 no-connect is not treated as an open drain output: each SOT-223 tab pad 4 is assigned the output net. U7's cached library identifier is LM393 but its value and SKU identify MAX9142; no LM393 open-collector model was assumed in assessing its push-pull comparator circuit. U5 NOR latch cross-coupling and grounded unused inputs were traced without asserting power-on latch state.

The five ERC errors were inspected: three un-driven power-input modeling reports (external ground/VMOT/ignition supplies), one missing explicit FLT no-connect intent, and two identically named module sensor-power output pins tied together. These are native ERC violations; they do not by themselves demonstrate missing physical supply or two independent regulators shorted. No circuit-blocking allegation is made from them. DRC's 13 error-level entries are marked excluded (three incomplete thermal reliefs and five connector mechanical holes each producing padstack/annular reports); no electrical open is inferred from them. The zero-unconnected result is not a waveform or circuit-function measurement.
