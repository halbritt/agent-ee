## Outcomes and locators

- Native schematic: R1 value `100k` at line 1274; C1 value `100n` at line 955. Native PCB repeats those values at lines 256 and 107. Component and pad identities agree across the native schematic, board, supplied XML export, and freshly exported `native-export.xml`.
- Native XML topology: `/IN` = J1.1/R1.1, `/OUT` = R1.2/C1.1/J1.2, GND = C1.2/J1.3. Thus the modeled series resistor and shunt capacitor correctly represent the saved design; omission of the ideal connector is appropriate.
- `audit-results.json` records independently parsed native data, hashes matching the packet, geometry, calculations, and decoded supplied waveform. R*C = 0.01 s. For the stated ideal step, Vout(1 ms) = 0.095162581964 V, 0.524837418036 V below the lower bound. Vout(5 ms) = 0.393469340287 V; no bound was stated at 5 ms.
- Supplied `work/rc-step/deck.cir`: 1 ns PWL rise, zero initial DC input, no load, and measurements at the requested 1 ms and 5 ms. Actual binary `work/rc-step/result.raw` contains 1025 real points. Independent interpolation yields 0.095162513912 V at 1 ms and 0.393469316277 V at 5 ms, agreeing with the supplied measurement log. The nanosecond rise and numerical interpolation errors are immaterial to the failed lower bound.
- `independent.cir` uses values from the fresh native export with a 1 us maximum integration step. `independent-ngspice.log`, under Measurements for Transient Analysis, gives vout_1ms = 9.516254e-02 V and vout_5ms = 3.934693e-01 V over 5022 points. Exit status alone was not used as evidence of circuit behavior.
- ERC and DRC JSON reports were inspected, not rerun: zero reported violations, zero DRC unconnected items, zero schematic-parity entries. This does not establish the transient requirement. Ignored checks remain omitted.
