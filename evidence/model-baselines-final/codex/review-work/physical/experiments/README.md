## Outcomes and adjudication

1. Supplied native DRC: 142 violations, 0 unconnected items, 114 schematic parity warnings. All 13 error-severity entries explicitly carry excluded=true and blank comments. They are three starved_thermal entries and five mechanical padstack entries plus five annular_width entries describing the same five holes. No count is treated as a behavioral test.
2. Independent DRC: 156 violations, 0 unconnected items, 114 parity warnings. The 13 error entries reproduce. Library coverage differs: this local rerun reports 129 missing-library entries, whereas the supplied check environment resolves stock libraries and reports 106 library footprint mismatches plus 9 missing-library entries. This difference is an environment limitation, not a newly discovered physical defect. See drc-comparison.json.
3. All 694 numbered PCB pad instances compared with native XML have identical net names. The remaining 25 pads are anonymous mechanical or paste geometry. This confirms nominal pad assignments, not fabricated continuity, component internals, or correct circuit behavior. Source hierarchy includes ten instances and byte-identical disposable VR sheet alias.
4. Five anonymous J1 holes at (71.95,35.49), (75.55,39.34), (120.00,28.49), (199.65,39.34), (203.25,35.49) mm are native PTH. Three have pad/drill diameter 4.0/4.0 mm; two have 2.2/2.2 mm. Circular nominal annulus=(pad diameter-drill diameter)/2=0.0 mm. Native DRC cites a 0.1 mm configured minimum, with explicit exclusions. Separate drill export puts all five in Polygonus-Universal-Base-PTH.drl (T10 and T12); NPTH file is empty. This supports a manufacturing concern about the plated/zero-ring representation. It does not establish a signal connection failure: these are no-net mounting holes, and connector/fabricator intent and finished-hole tolerances are not established.
5. Starved relief entries: C15.2, U7.4, C14.2 have one native F.Cu zone relief spoke versus configured minimum two; all three are excluded. Each also has explicit ground trace connections (0.25 mm) toward nearby ground vias. See exact track UUIDs in thermal-track-connections.json and thermal-reliefs.png. Therefore the zone-spoke warning alone does not demonstrate a floating ground, inadequate return, or thermal failure, and is not raised as an electrical defect.
6. CAN2: native PCB and schematic connect J1.15/J2.73/U20.7/U21.7/R36.1/R37.1 to CAN2+, and corresponding J1.11/J2.74/U20.6/U21.6/R36.2/R37.2 to CAN2-. R36 and R37 are both 120 ohm DNP. Native netlist retains DNP pin connectivity; it must not be mistaken for populated termination. Selected bus track total lengths are approximately 252.43/252.40 mm, including all branches; these totals are not endpoint path lengths or impedance measurements. No donor-only model establishes module/harness termination, populated end-to-end resistance, transceiver signal integrity, or dynamic operation. Absence of populated carrier termination is not sufficient to assert failure with unspecified module/harness circuitry.
7. Saved stackup declares four copper layers and 0.035 mm nominal copper per layer. Selected 12 V, ground and heater paths were inventoried in board-measurements.json. Width and via counts were not converted to ampacity or thermal qualification: operating currents, simultaneity, copper tolerances, ambient, enclosure cooling and allowed rise were not established. No load stimulus was invented.
8. Full ERC was read for coverage and error categories. Power-drive annotation and duplicate power-output types do not independently prove absent supply or contention inside modular circuitry. The 353-entry ERC report is not an operating circuit test; not every warning received component-specific adjudication in this physical review.
