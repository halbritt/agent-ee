# Half duplex RS-485 breakout

Make a small 3.3 V half-duplex RS-485 transceiver board for a host UART. Expose
VCC, GND, TX, RX, driver enable and receiver enable on a host header. Expose A, B
and ground on the bus connector. Fit a 120 ohm termination resistor and label
this as an end-of-bus board. Include local supply bypassing and choose stocked
parts. Keep the outline compact; no galvanic isolation is required. Signal
integrity, bus polarity interoperability and fault behavior remain bench tests.
