# Native prerequisite

The unmodified RC schematic and every net must match this saved PCB. Full native
DRC must establish connectivity/isolation before the copper bound is evaluated.
Removing the B.Cu /IN segment must leave the DC check unsupported/unknown.

# Analytic bound

The /IN trace between J1.1 at (100,100) mm and R1.1 at (104.0875,100) mm is
4.0875 mm long and 0.25 mm wide: 3.0875 mm F.Cu and 1 mm B.Cu. Two 0.3 mm
through vias join the layers. J1.1 has a 1 mm plated hole. At explicit
rho=1.724e-8 ohm m, 35um copper, 20um barrel plating, 1.6mm height and 1A
total positive injection, the ideal sum bound before rounding allowance is:

- Trace: 0.008053542857142857 ohm.
- Each via: 0.001567903553659586 ohm.
- J1.1 barrel: 0.00044797244390273887 ohm.
- Total: 0.011637322408364768 V, below the 0.012 V limit.

These constants were calculated with independent 45-digit decimal arithmetic,
using circular annulus area pi*t*(diameter-t), treating the nominal hole as its
outer diameter. They are not generated by the implementation. Every conductor
on other nets is inventoried as excluded. Reducing the maximum to 0.010 V must
produce a defect, with identical native board and DRC results.

The model assumes ideal pad/junction contacts. The bound is for that declared
model and does not certify pad spreading, solder/package resistance, self-heating
or production material/dimension minima. This fixture makes no fabrication claim.
