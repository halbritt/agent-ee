
### inputs/requirements/fixture-requirements.md
1: # Declared fixture requirements (RC low-pass review exercise)
2: 
3: These are artificial review requirements declared for this demonstration
4: fixture. They are not ECU electrical requirements, and they are fixed for the
5: whole demonstration (recorded before any review run).
6: 
7: 1. Step response: with an ideal 1 V step at /IN and no external load, the
8:    voltage at /OUT at t = 1 ms after the step must satisfy
9:    0.62 V <= V(/OUT, 1 ms) <= 0.65 V.
10:    (Intended design: R1 = 10 kohm, C1 = 100 nF, tau = 1 ms,
11:    V(1 ms) = 1 - exp(-1) = 0.6321 V.)
12: 2. Geometry: every copper track segment on the board must be at least
13:    0.20 mm wide. This is a declared geometric requirement for the exercise,
14:    not an ampacity or manufacturing guarantee.
15: 
16: Scope limits: the ideal R/C simulation establishes only the unloaded step
17: response. Tolerance, temperature, loading, ADC sampling, EMI and hardware
18: behavior are untested by this fixture.
