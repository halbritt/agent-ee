# Disposable RC fixture baseline

R-native: The captured fixture must pass declared ERC and DRC checks, and its
netlist export must complete. Export establishes connectivity evidence, not
analog behavior.

R-width: Saved copper tracks must be at least 0.20 mm wide. This geometric
exercise criterion does not establish ampacity or fabrication qualification.

R-response: With an ideal unloaded 1 V step at /IN, /OUT at 1 ms must be
between 0.62 and 0.65 V inclusive. This fixture does not establish temperature,
tolerance, loaded response, or physical prototype performance.
