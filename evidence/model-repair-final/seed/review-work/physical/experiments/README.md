Reproduce with `python3 /work/experiments/audit.py`.

Checks and assumptions: compare direct native R/C values against a fresh native XML export and supplied check records; enumerate every saved segment; inspect original outline, footprints, pads, via and saved ground polygon. The analytic RC calculation assumes the explicitly required ideal unloaded 1 V step and initially discharged capacitor. Initial step current and peak resistor power are stimulus calculations only, not part qualification. The plane continuity probe samples a route at intervals no greater than 0.005 mm and explicitly starts/ends on plated annuli outside drills. It corroborates connectivity but does not establish resistance, impedance, temperature rise or manufacturability. No candidate scratch edit is claimed to fix anything.

Outcomes: audit command exited 0 with empty stderr. Native schematic/board hashes match packet hashes. The fresh XML nets match the supplied XML: J1.1-R1.1 is /IN, R1.2-C1.1-J1.2 is /OUT, and C1.2-J1.3 is GND. All five F.Cu segments are 0.25 mm, meeting the 0.20 mm geometric requirement. The four Edge.Cuts lines form a closed 20 by 12 mm rectangle; the three footprints fit within it with no apparent courtyard overlap. The OUT branch terminates on the R1-C1 track at (107.5,100) mm. C1.2 has a direct F.Cu trace to the via at (113,100) mm; the B.Cu saved polygon includes all sampled via-annulus points and four diagonal J1.3 thermal connections. The 3780-point sampled route from J1.3's annulus to the via's annulus lies on ground copper throughout. The new KiCad DRC reports zero violations, unconnected items and parity issues (inspect report arrays as well as exit status).

Both native files specify R1=100k and C1=100n. The independently calculated tau is 10 ms, Vout(1 ms)=0.095162581964 V, and Vout(5 ms)=0.393469340287 V. The required 1 ms band corresponds to tau=0.952542..1.033502 ms. Supplied `work/rc-step/ngspice.log` measures 0.09516251 V and 0.3934693 V, agreeing with the calculation. The deck begins with a zero-volt input, ramps to 1 V over 1 ns, has no external load and runs through 5 ms; the finite rise is far too short to explain the failure. This is a response requirement violation, not a tool execution failure. No additional evidence-supported physical defect was found.
