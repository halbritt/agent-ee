Analytical result in shutdown-calculation.json: tau=R*C=10 ms. For t>=0, KCL gives C*dVout/dt+Vout/R=0, hence Vout=1*exp(-t/tau). At 5 ms, Vout=0.6065306597 V, exceeding 0.01 V. Reaching 0.01 V takes 46.05170186 ms. The requirement would need tau <=1.085736205 ms; that is a derived constraint, not an unstated requirement.

These agree with the closed-form result. shutdown.tsv preserves the trace and shutdown.log preserves actual measurements. Success status alone was not treated as evidence of function.
