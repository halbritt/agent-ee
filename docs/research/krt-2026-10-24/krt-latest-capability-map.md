## Findings that govern integration

1. **Required coverage must be enforced outside KRT's aggregate verdicts.** `check_complete` permits DONE with named UNGRADED components; several geometry/assembly findings are advisory or baseline-relative. Native DRC/ERC and required engineering checks still govern accepted artifacts.
2. **Preserve authored rules and geometry deliberately.** Defaults can narrow geometry and lower output project floors. Severity relaxation is separate opt-in, not the default. `--no-fix-drc-settings --escalation off --strict-sizes` are useful controls, but they do not prove full compliance or universal immutability. The cap-repair command has different clearance/write semantics; short pad necks can remain; native final grading is mandatory.
3. **Placement constraints are only partly connected to optimization and later repair.** Functional groups, declared faces, anchors, pin relationships and legality are substantive; arrays/replication/angles/keep-away have distinct application or grading paths. Templates can be displaced by final legalization. Routing and cap repair can move footprints and must trigger a complete final placement grade.
4. **Planes are a multi-stage commitment.** Pouring is not routing. Plane nets must be included in the final route scope. Pour-served BGA balls require exact final fill verification; some repair paths still return zero with incomplete pads.
5. **Preserving rules can lose KRT metadata unless handled separately.** `route.py`'s CLI protection/impedance persistence is under the DRC-writeback conditional; the wrapper must retain commitments while refusing unauthorized rule changes.
6. **Plan replay is useful but is not qualified resumption.** Headless snapshots contain PCB copper without per-step project/rule siblings. Selected steps are manual replay. Timeout behavior differs from explicit stop; safe cancellation, complete checkpoint custody and successful resume must be demonstrated.
7. **KRT is a subsystem, not the whole engineering product.** The source map does not establish component selection, circuit correctness, native schematic generation or fabrication/assembly package validation. Those remain agent-ee acceptance work.
