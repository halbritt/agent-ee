# USB powered GPIO supply

Make a compact USB-C powered board that supplies regulated 3.3 V at up to 100 mA
from a standard 5 V USB source. No USB data or power delivery negotiation is
needed. Present 3.3 V, ground and four independent signal pass-through pairs on
labelled headers so a host can wire four GPIO signals through the board. Provide
the USB-C sink configuration and local input/output bypass capacitors. Choose
stocked assembly parts and a practical outline. Check output voltage and load
regulation on hardware before use.
