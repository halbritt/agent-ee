          ST suggests to insert a resistor (Rprot) in line to prevent the microcontroller I/O pins from

                                               0 .7                  (V OH μ C − V IH )

          •      Ilatchup > 20 mA
          •      VOHµC > 4.5 V
          •      35 Ω ≤ Rprot ≤ 100 KΩ
          Then, the recommended value is Rprot = 1 KΩ
