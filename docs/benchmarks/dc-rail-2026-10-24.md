## Failure and correction

The existing `agent-ee.dc-path/1` rejected the actual acquisition board with
`unmodeled conductor class`. It accepted only an isolated F.Cu endpoint network
with two round SMD terminals. The actual unchanged board contains 209 tracks/vias
and 49 pads; power and ground use both copper layers and plated holes.

The installed kicad-happy extractor was inspected for reuse. Its default copper,
plating and temperature values, missing-geometry skips and per-net approximations
could not supply a qualified bound. The new contract version 2 explicitly declares
the physical inputs and ideal contacts. It sums all relevant conductor resistances
and requires full native connectivity/isolation and schematic parity on the same
captured board. Native raw findings, ignored conductive checks, incomplete routes,
stale results and unsupported geometry cannot produce a pass. No circuit, native
rule severity or acquisition placement/routing output was changed.

fresh native processes, matching production extraction, and the same four-layer

These whole-net bounds deliberately give the same value for terminals on the
same net. All 258 native objects are accounted for: 171 on the declared rails
(including ideal contacts) and 87 explicitly excluded other-net objects. The
combined ideal supply/return bound is approximately 5.1072 mV.

The diagnostic declares 25 °C, rho=1.7578766e-8 Ωm, 35 μm copper, 1.6 mm barrel
height, 20 μm plating, unit width/hole factors and at most 20 mA total positive
injection per net. Those are explicit test conditions, **not verified production
minima or a closed operating envelope**. The individual 10 mV diagnostic limits
do not replace the authored 3.3 V ±2% system requirement. Pad spreading, solder,
package resistance and self-heating remain outside this ideal model. Required
physical bounds, component limitations and full manufacturing acceptance stay open.

## Reproduction and limits

The supported native image is `agent-ee:exec-b5ff7ad833e5ea01`, KiCad 10.0.7;
the exact Python/pcbnew hashes are in the captured qualification files. Run the
committed control as documented in [dc-path.md](../dc-path.md). Native regressions:

```sh
ae-ci-exec . -- python3 scripts/strict_suite.py --jobs 2 \
  --summary build/dc-qualified.md test_dc_rail test_dc_path
ae-ci-exec . -- python3 scripts/strict_suite.py --jobs 3 \
  --summary build/dc-integration.md test_capabilities test_capabilities_structure \
  test_capability_pipeline test_measurements test_baseline
```

Zones, copper graphics, slotted rail holes and non-through vias refuse rather
than disappear from the model. Zone support remains an unmet whole-system
capability. This work does not establish KRT parity for plane resistance,
return-path analysis, impedance or routing, and claims no superiority over KRT.
No complete manufacturing package is accepted by this report.
