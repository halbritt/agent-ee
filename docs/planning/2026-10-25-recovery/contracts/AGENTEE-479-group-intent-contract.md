## 1. Decision Summary

1. **One Supported Contract (`agent-ee.placement-intent/2`) with Raw-Byte Preservation**:
   - `placement_intent.validate(intent, groups)` and `placement_job.py` accept both retained M1 qualification input dialects directly from their unmodified source bytes:
     - **KRT floorplan-intent v1 (`esp_prog`)**: `kind == "floorplan-intent"`, `schema == 1`, `min_reader == 12`, `units == "mm"`, `board == "board.kicad_pcb"`, paired with `groups.json` (`schema == 1`, `board == "board.kicad_pcb"`).
     - **Agent-EE placement-intent v1 / legacy unversioned (`acquisition`)**: `schema == "agent-ee.placement-intent/1"` or unversioned `{edge_connectors, fixed_poses, proximity, electrical_locality}` paired with `groups.json`.
   - Raw original bytes (`intent.json` `sha256:320cf961...`, `groups.json` `sha256:d48e2a5d...`) are never stripped, renamed, or replaced by a `.stripped.json` substitute (`REC-02`, `RD-02`).
2. **Four Explicit Field Dispositions (Zero Silent Drops)**:
   - Every field in `intent` and `groups` is classified in `report['intent_contract']` as:
     - `enforced_hard`: admission, native capture, solver feasibility, and final saved-board native gate (`board`, `envelope`, `assembly.sides`, `legality_budget.oob_count`, `fixed_poses`, `edge_connectors` identity/side/setback/overhang/orientation, complete footprint partition, sourced `electrical_locality`, intra-group connected-peer cohesion, and group bounding-box non-intrusion).
     - `enforced_search_and_verified_report`: active Rust search objective term (`crates/placement-engine/src/job.rs`) plus mandatory final saved-board native measurement and report (`proximity` with `basis: "pad_edge"` and `context.limit: "derived_default"`, inter-group `groups[].near` adjacency distances, and group member-to-anchor spread).
     - `validated_empty`: explicitly verified to be empty (`blocks: []`, `keepouts: []`, `must_lock: []`, `decaps: {}`); any non-empty entry raises `JobRefusal` until M3 floorplan/zone handlers land (`REC-01`, `RD-01`).
     - `retained_descriptive_context`: schema-checked and recorded in `intent_contract.source_context` with `executes: false` (`defaults`, `dispositions`, `context`, `why`, `note`).
3. **`pad_edge` Proximity Is Evaluated on Native Pad Copper Edges, Never Chebyshev Pad Centres**:
   - `esp_prog` declares three crystal/load-cap `proximity` rules with `basis: "pad_edge"` (`Y1->U1` `8.1 mm`, `C2->Y1` `2.2 mm`, `C4->Y1` `2.2 mm`) and `context.limit: "derived_default"` (75th-percentile human corpus from `placement_survey.py`).
   - `_capture_native` attaches each pad's local copper bounding box `rect_local_nm` (`_pad_copper`) to `problem.footprints[].pads[]`, `job.rs` evaluates `ProximityBasis::PadEdge` as Euclidean box-edge distance between transformed pad copper bounds (steering search), and `_check_proximity_native` grades final saved native pad copper polygons (`TransformShapeToPolygon(..., ERROR_OUTSIDE)`).
   - Per `RD-04`, `context.limit == "derived_default"` is graded and reported on final coordinates (`pass` when $\le \text{max\_mm}$, `advisory_exceeded` / `info` if exceeded), whereas a sourced engineering requirement (`acquisition` `spec.md` `3.0 mm` `electrical_locality`) is enforced as a hard defect.

## 4. Exact Geometry, Rotation, and Functional-Group Predicates

### 4.1 `proximity` with `basis: "pad_edge"` vs. Pad-Centre Locality
1. **Why `pad_edge` Must Not Map to Chebyshev Pad-Centre**:
   - In `esp_prog` (`build/agent545/inputs/intent.json`), all three `proximity` entries declare `"basis": "pad_edge"` and `"context": {"limit": "derived_default", "source": "placement.crystals"}`:
     - `Y1 -> U1` (`pads: {"U1": ["9", "10"], "Y1": ["1", "2"]}`, `max_mm: 8.1`)
     - `C2 -> Y1` (`pads: {"C2": ["1"], "Y1": ["2"]}`, `max_mm: 2.2`)
     - `C4 -> Y1` (`pads: {"C4": ["1"], "Y1": ["1"]}`, `max_mm: 2.2`)
   - On the retained `esp_prog` final board (`final-a1/board.kicad_pcb`, `sha256:dfffc042...`), the three metrics diverge materially because SMD pads have finite copper dimensions:
     - `Y1 -> U1`: Chebyshev pad-centre = `5.3805 mm`; Euclidean pad-centre = `6.9347 mm` (`U1.9 <-> Y1.1`) / `8.3180 mm` (`U1.10 <-> Y1.2`); **Euclidean `pad_edge`** = `5.4237 mm` min (`U1.9 <-> Y1.1`) / `6.7888 mm` max connected leg (`U1.10 <-> Y1.2`), both $\le 8.1000\text{ mm}$.
     - `C2 -> Y1` (`C2.1 <-> Y1.2`): Chebyshev pad-centre = `2.0920 mm`; Euclidean pad-centre = `2.3407 mm` ($> 2.2\text{ mm}$); **Euclidean `pad_edge` = `1.1553 mm` ($\le 2.2000\text{ mm}$)**.
     - `C4 -> Y1` (`C4.1 <-> Y1.1`): Chebyshev pad-centre = `2.2000 mm`; Euclidean pad-centre = `3.0359 mm` ($> 2.2\text{ mm}$); **Euclidean `pad_edge` = `1.7492 mm` ($\le 2.2000\text{ mm}$)**.
2. **Exact `pad_edge` Definition in Search and Final Native Verification**:
   - **Capture (`_capture_native`)**: Each pad in `row['pads']` carries `rect_local_nm: [x0, y0, x1, y1]` from `_pad_copper(pad, origin, q)['rect_nm']`. When a multi-pad `proximity` entry (`Y1 -> U1`) spans multiple nets, candidate pad pairs are filtered to same-net pad pairs (`Y1.1` on `Net-(C4-Pad1)` with `U1.9`; `Y1.2` on `Net-(C2-Pad1)` with `U1.10`) so every active signal leg is bounded.
   - **Rust Search (`job.rs` `ProximityBasis::PadEdge`)**: For each paired pad `(pa, pb)`, place local copper boxes $A = \text{place\_rect}(\text{pa.rect\_local}, \text{pose}_a)$ and $B = \text{place\_rect}(\text{pb.rect\_local}, \text{pose}_b)$, compute $\Delta x = \max(0, A_{x0}-B_{x1}, B_{x0}-A_{x1})$, $\Delta y = \max(0, A_{y0}-B_{y1}, B_{y0}-A_{y1})$, and penalize $\max(0, \lceil \sqrt{\Delta x^2 + \Delta y^2} \rceil - \text{max\_nm})$ across the paired legs.
   - **Final Native Check (`_check_proximity_native`)**: In a fresh native process on the saved board, extract each pad's outer copper polygon (`pad.TransformShapeToPolygon(..., pcbnew.ERROR_OUTSIDE)`) and compute exact 2D polygon edge-to-edge Euclidean distance (`segment_distance2` in `placement_predicates.py:191-195`). Report `status: "pass"` when all pairs satisfy $d_{\text{edge}} \le \text{max\_mm}$, `"info"` (`advisory_exceeded`) if a `derived_default` corpus limit is exceeded (`RD-04`), and `"failed"` if a sourced engineering requirement (`limit != "derived_default"`) is exceeded.

### 4.2 Acquired Native Rotations vs. Declared Profile
1. **Explicit Profile Rotation (`envelope.fixed_footprints[].rotation_deg` or `fixed_poses[].rot`)**:
   - When the profile declares an explicit rotation (`acquisition` `J1: 0°`, `J2: 180°`, `J3: 0°`; `esp_prog` `Ref*: 0.0°`, `Ref*~2: 0.0°`), `_capture_native` and `materialize` verify $\text{quarter\_turns}(\text{native}) == \text{quarter\_turns}(\text{declared})$ and lock `rotations = [q]`.
2. **Preserved Native Edge-Connector Orientation (`intent.edge_connectors` without explicit angle)**:
   - On `esp_prog`, `USB1` (`west`, `in_plane` edge receptacle) has acquired native rotation `180.0°` (`q = 2`, facing west), and `CON1`/`CON2` (`east`, `top_mount` / `perpendicular_top` 1x6 and 1x7 headers) have acquired native rotation `0.0°` (`q = 0`, pin row parallel to the east edge).
   - `_capture_native` neither assumes an acquired rotation is wrong nor allows `edge_connectors` to rotate freely into an inward-facing orientation: it sets `rotations = [initial_q]`, records `rotation_authority: "preserved_native_orientation"`, and `_check_edge_intent_native` verifies on the final saved board that `saved_quarter_turns == initial_quarter_turns`, `saved_side == declared_side`, `nearest_edge == declared_edge`, `setback_nm <= max_setback_nm`, and `overhang_nm` lies within `overhang_mm`.

### 4.3 Functional Grouping, `groups[].near`, and Final-Coordinate Cohesion Predicate
Per `RD-04` and `groups.json` (`reset.why`: *"No unsupported geometric distance is imposed"*; `intent.dispositions.rules.decap_distance`: *"Do not derive a hard distance from a pile"*), no universal `1.5 mm` or `3.0 mm` threshold is invented. Instead, functional grouping is enforced by a **parameter-free topological/geometric coordinate predicate** (`check_group_intent`) backed by three search terms in `job.rs`:
1. **Hard Final-Coordinate Group Predicates (`status: "failed"` $\rightarrow$ `eligibility: "INELIGIBLE"`)**:
   - **P1 — Complete Native Footprint Partition & Side Compliance**: Every native footprint on the saved board is partitioned into `groups.json` (`16` on `esp_prog`, `17` on `acquisition`), `intent.fixed_poses` (`2` fiducials on `esp_prog`), or verified padless `silk_only` graphics (`3` on `esp_prog`), with `saved_side` matching `assembly.sides` (`"F"`).
   - **P2 — Intra-Group Connected-Peer Cohesion (`intra_group_subpair_inversion == []`)**: For every passive/support member $m \in G$ connected by a 2-terminal intra-group net to a peer $p \in G$ (e.g., `R1.1 <-> Q1.1` on `Net-(Q1-Pad1)` and `R2.1 <-> Q2.1` on `Net-(Q2-Pad1)` in `reset`; `C1.1 <-> U2.2` on `Net-(C1-Pad1)` in `power`; `R3.2 <-> U1.3` and `R4.2 <-> U1.4` in `core`; `C2.1 <-> Y1.2` and `C4.1 <-> Y1.1` in `core`), the Euclidean centre distance $d(m, p)$ on final saved coordinates must satisfy $d(m, p) < \min_{G' \neq G} d(m, \text{anchor}(G'))$, and for distinct sub-channels in the same group (`Q1` vs `Q2` in `reset`), $d(R_i, Q_i) < d(R_i, Q_{j \neq i})$.
   - **P3 — Group Bounding-Box Non-Intrusion (`bbox_intruders == []`)**: For each group $G$, let $B(G) = [\min_{m \in G} x_m, \min_{m \in G} y_m, \max_{m \in G} x_m, \max_{m \in G} y_m]$ be the axis-aligned bounding box of its member origins on the same mounting side. No anchor or movable circuit member of a distinct group $G' \neq G$ may lie inside $B(G)$.
2. **Active Search Terms + Mandatory Final Coordinate Report (`enforced_search_and_verified_report`)**:
   - **`job.rs` `group_terms` Upgrade**:
     1. *Intra-group anchor + connected-peer Manhattan tethers*: sum Manhattan distances from each member $m \in G$ to `anchor(G)` AND to each intra-group peer $p \in G$ sharing an internal net with $m$ (weighted $2\times$ for 2-terminal intra-group nets so `R1` tracks `Q1`, `R2` tracks `Q2`, and `C1`/`C3` track `U2`).
     2. *Group bounding-box intrusion penalty*: penalize any foreign group footprint $f \notin G$ whose origin lies inside $B(G)$ by its Manhattan depth inside $B(G)$.
